What actually moves your numbers

Gnōthi compares your days with each other, not with population averages. Each link shows how strong the evidence is, and anything uncertain can be tested as an experiment.

Method: days split at the median of each factor, difference in means, Welch's t-test. These are correlations. Use an experiment to check cause.

Test it on yourself

Change one thing for 7 days. Gnōthi compares those days with the 14 days before, and tells you whether the change made a real difference.
